Real yields hold the long end high as CPI arrives

Since Sep 1 the 10Y has moved +45 bp, all of it real yield (+47 bp); the breakeven moved −2 bp even with Brent settling at $104.72.

Rates are the week's organizing force, and so far they are a real-rate story. The 10Y par yield ended Friday at 5.24%, a few basis points under Monday's 5.31%, and the 30Y at 5.60%. New supply has repriced with it: last week's 10-year auction cleared at 5.30% and the 30-year at 5.62%, against 4.83% and 5.31% in September.
