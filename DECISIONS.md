# Decisions

- Deterministic collection happens before AI synthesis.
- `OBSERVED`, `INTERPRETATION`, and `WATCH` remain separate.
- Cuttingboard is optional, read-only context and never writable authority.
- PRE-MARKET is the first shipping cadence.
- Local and simple comes before services or frameworks.
- Signal over plumbing: the brief carries conclusions and useful tables; collapsed ledgers carry proof.
- A commissioning run names the market phase it actually collected in (resolved from the exchange clock), never a hardcoded checkpoint.
- Same-day post-close runs may use daily history through the prior completed session while the provider has not published today's bar; that lag is visible in coverage, never fills TODAY, and never applies beyond that day.
- Only LIVE runs publish; fictional replay output never reaches the public brief.
- The synthesis contract asks for one short headline claim, one global coverage caveat, and no table restatement; the validator enforces the headline bound and fail-closed grounding unchanged.
- The model receives the bounded synthesis projection (one copy of each admitted fact, legend, leadership IDs, attention, compact sources, coverage, schema); the full normalized record stays local and `--full-packet` reproduces the original payload for diagnostics.
- Deterministic code owns every persistent continuity identity (watch, relationship, comparison, snapshot ref); the analyst proposes new items and assessments and never names or renames a carried record.
- Saved artifacts separate deterministic observed/comparable change (`observed`) from model-authored assessment (`assessment`, class INTERPRETATION); the point-in-time factual record stays usable without the interpretation.
- Premarket admits only the previous exchange session's accepted close handoff; intraday editions admit this session's premarket anchor and latest accepted edition. Anything else is an explicit cold start.
- A post-close run may admit labeled PROVISIONAL session-ending prints (final fifteen minutes before the close, within ninety minutes after it) so a cron-delayed close can still hand off; the handoff and its closing character are marked provisional. Without any session observation the run records EARLIER_HISTORY_ONLY and does not advance the close handoff; the next morning says close continuity is unavailable.
- GitHub Actions artifacts are short-horizon operational continuity (thirty days), not historical storage; artifact contracts are versioned and hashed so a permitted archive can adopt them later.
- One configured analyst for every edition; identity, route, budget profile, and usage are recorded per run. No provider registry.
- The configured analyst carries one bounded fallback (`fallback_model`, Fable 5). A synthesis checkpoint attempts the primary and, only when OpenRouter reports it has no eligible endpoint (the explicit "no endpoints found" 404, a routing failure before any billing), makes exactly one fallback call; a bare or unrelated 404, transient, auth, malformed, and local-grounding failures never fall back, an environment override carries no fallback, and each run records the primary failure (sanitized, never the raw provider body), whether fallback occurred, and the model that served. OpenRouter's own `models` array is deliberately not used: a "no endpoints" 404 halts that chain rather than advancing it, and it would hide the primary failure and could bill twice.
- Light editions receive a deterministically selected context and can cite only what they were shown; nothing is truncated to fit a budget.
- Rich interpretation is scarce: the premarket edition and the opening-structure update (open +30 minutes) are the day's only analyst calls. Open +1M, the hourly refreshes and the close are deterministic: they refresh the observed record under the last accepted interpretation, frozen as a hashed continuity record, and never rewrite it. The standalone rich CLOSE_1M edition is removed rather than fitted to a larger budget.
- Checkpoints are anchored to the exchange session, never to a Pacific wall clock: premarket at open −30 minutes, open +1 and +30 minutes, hourly refreshes at exchange-clock hours, the close at close +1 minute. The exchange calendar is the only authority; times are displayed in Pacific time and therefore move with New York's seasons (the opening-structure update reads 7:00 AM PT in New York daylight time and 8:00 AM PT in standard time now that British Columbia keeps UTC−7).
- A synthesis checkpoint is due only within its own short window (`schedule.TOLERANCE_MINUTES`, twenty minutes) so the wake that naturally follows it is its one attempt; the :31 UTC wake that exists for the other New York season lands thirty-one minutes late and resolves to SKIP, never a second paid attempt, whatever the first attempt's outcome. Deterministic refreshes and the close keep the forty-five-minute window.
- A watch's criteria keep the values their author saw: the rows a criterion quotes are frozen with the watch when it is created and travel with it; a later synthesis may reassess or replace the watch, a deterministic refresh may not, and newer market data never changes a carried threshold.
- The close snapshot hands the session off from deterministic state: closing snapshots from its own evidence, the carried assessment labeled with the checkpoint that interpreted it. A close without a same-session interpretation still hands off (no page); a close or refresh without observations from the session publishes nothing and leaves the last accepted page and bundle untouched.
- Every page has one clock line ending in the scheduler's next update. A synthesis edition reads "As of" its run time; a carried edition names both clocks in that same line, "Analysis anchored" (when the interpretation it carries was made) and "Observed record refreshed" (when this run refreshed the observed record). The run time is not a claim that every datum is that fresh: each row keeps its own observation clock or date. Frozen prose cites the rows the analyst saw at their own clock, tables cite this run's rows.
- A current print's reader label is presentation, read from that row's own `observed_at` against the session's open and close: "Premarket", "Intraday" or "After-hours vs prior close", "Near-close vs prior close" for a PROVISIONAL print, and "Latest trade vs prior close" when the phase cannot be placed in this session or a table's prints span phases. One helper (`render.current_print_label`) supplies it to every path (markers, table proofs and captions, ledger, figures). A frozen row keeps the phase it was observed in; nothing is labeled as the official close. The metric string (`premarket return`), evidence ID, identity key and comparisons are unchanged.
- Current availability is owned by this run's deterministic packet and source state: missing domains, coverage limitations, the Sources drawer and the evidence rows. The analyst's `banner.limitation` is point-in-time interpretation; it renders, with its marker, only on the synthesis edition that wrote it and is withheld, never edited, on carried editions.
- Reader surfaces use reader words; internal contract vocabulary (metric strings, statuses, checkpoint names, source reasons) stays valid in Technical details and the saved artifacts. The coverage list says `not collected` where the packet says `not automated`, and names market sections an edition could not fill as "Not in this edition"; optional Cuttingboard absence is not an editorial omission, and Technical details state its status and reason. A carried watch reads "From an earlier read". A paragraph's uncertainty and alternative explanation render as separate "Caveat:" and "Could also be:" lines, never one unlabeled sentence.
- Visible absence vocabulary is three terms: `no print` (current observation missing, history present), `—` (structurally not applicable), `not collected` (source not automated). A displayed value that rounds to zero is an unsigned neutral zero.
- The analyst selects attention triggers through one surface only: `attention[]`, each item an admitted deterministic trigger `id` with a `why`; deterministic code derives the selected IDs from those items for the frozen interpretation, and unknown or repeated IDs fail closed. The former `attention_ids` list was a second model-owned copy of the same decision that the provider's strict grammar could not tie to `attention[]`; production run 35347858192 (PREMARKET, 2026-09-18) returned three IDs and an empty list, schema-valid on the wire and impossible locally, so the duplicate authority was removed rather than the equality check loosened.
- The narrative contract is `market-brief.narrative.v2`: v1 plus a required `take` after `summary` (`text` with a 160-character editorial target, class INTERPRETATION, up to four evidence IDs). The take is the single most useful interpretation in the brief that its analyst could turn out to be wrong about: one short sentence, not a prediction and not trade advice. It is empty (no text, no evidence) when the evidence is too thin, and text without evidence or evidence without text fails closed. It rules an alternative out only when supplied evidence shows the alternative false: missing data (news, breadth, anything not collected) rules nothing out. It is a current-condition record like the headline and the read: supplied current evidence only (no prior `anchor:` refs, no `cmp-` comparison IDs), grounded numeric placeholders, no literal numbers, the SAMPLE current-language rule, and the trade-language rule with one narrow exemption for the market noun "sell-off" ("sell-offs", "selloff"); "buy", "sell" as an action, entries, targets, sizing, execution and orders still reject. The rows it cites freeze with the interpretation, so a carried take shows the values and clocks its analyst saw while the tables show this run's. It renders as one "The take:" line after the read on synthesis and carried pages, with the existing evidence marker and no new styling, and renders nothing when empty. Continuity changes only in which rows freeze: no take in edition state, carried state or the close handoff, and no continuity or bundle schema change; an interpretation frozen under v1 still renders on the same session's refreshes.
- Voice is a positive prompt contract (synthesis v0.3): a sharp desk colleague writing to one discretionary trader building macro intuition, claim first, one idea per sentence, exact figures only through evidence placeholders and only when they sharpen the point, a mechanism only when the evidence supports one, the important blind spot stated once. Prompt provenance stays the prompt hash; there is no prompt-version field. Each synthesis edition records advisory voice telemetry in `metadata.style` (counts of eight contract words in reader prose, numeric placeholders, whether the take is present, its length, and whether it repeats the headline). It is observation only: it never affects acceptance, publication, a retry or the call count, which stays two analyst calls a day.
- The analyst reads its output contract in the same factored `$defs`/`$ref` form that `response_format` enforces (owner ruling 2026-09-25, reversing the PR #30 choice to keep the user-message copy inlined). The inlined copy cost 3,191 bytes per request, and with the take it put the real 2026-09-24 opening-structure context 26 bytes over the 40,000-byte light budget. The factored copy expands exactly to the inlined transport contract, so no field, bound description, enum, requirement or validation rule changes; the budgets and the light projection are unchanged. The isolated Claude CLI route still enforces the inlined form through `--json-schema`.
- The provider-enforced schema stays inside the envelope Anthropic has compiled fresh and accepted (5,295 bytes, 72 nodes, 11 objects, 55 properties; 2026-09-18). The first live check of the take (2026-09-25, 5,497 bytes, 75 nodes, 12 objects, 58 properties) was rejected with HTTP 400 before generation; every other request difference was ruled out and an open-source grammar compiler ranks all four schemas ever sent in the order Anthropic accepted and rejected them. The fix is transport-only: each paragraph array states its items' text bound once ("Each text: At most N characters"), so the summary and section paragraphs compile as one rule; every bound is still described and enforced locally. Every OpenRouter HTTP error recorded with its body (all but authentication failures, including a no-endpoint primary failure kept in provenance) now includes the provider's own bounded, printable error message from OpenRouter's `metadata.raw`, and nothing else of the provider body.
